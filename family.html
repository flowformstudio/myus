Questions

MyUS Family FAQ

Can I add friends instead of relatives?

Yes. Family is just the name. Add friends, neighbors, roommates, partners, relatives — anyone you're comfortable shipping with.

Does it cost extra to add someone?

No. MyUS Family is included free with your MyUS membership, on every plan.

Does everyone get their own MyUS login?

Not currently. People you add can shop and send packages to your suite, but the account — and its shipments — stays managed by you as the primary member.

Who pays for shipping?

You do, through your MyUS account — MyUS never bills the people you add. How your group splits the cost afterward is up to you, which is why MyUS Family works best between people who trust each other.

How many people can I add?

You can add as many people as you like, and edit or remove them at any time.

Can I remove or edit someone later?

Yes. You can update a member's details or remove them from your account whenever you need to.

Can everyone shop from different stores?

Absolutely. Everyone shops independently from the US stores they prefer — packages simply arrive at the same MyUS suite.

How does package consolidation work?

When packages arrive at your suite, you choose eligible ones to combine into a single international shipment — usually reducing the total shipping cost compared to sending them separately.
