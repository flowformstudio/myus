MyUS Family FAQ

Does it really cost nothing?

Really. MyUS Family is included free with every membership plan. You never pay to add a member.

Do they have to be actual family?

No — "Family" is just what we call it. Add friends, roommates, neighbors, a business partner: anyone you trust to ship with.

Who pays for shipments?

The account owner initiates shipments and pays MyUS; how the group splits the cost is up to you. Add people you trust to pay their share.

Is there a limit to how many people I can add?

Add as many people as you like — and remove anyone, anytime, from your account settings.
