Common questions

How does my MyUS online account work?

You can use your MyUS online account to:

  • Check if your merchandise has been received
  • Create a ship request
  • Check the status of your shipments
  • Change your account details such as credit card, shipping address and membership information

Note: You will receive an email notification when you receive a package, or when MyUS sends a shipment to you.

How do I use my MyUS addresses?

Enter your US or UK MyUS address as the "Shipping Address." It can always be found in the upper-right corner of your account.

When you buy from US online stores, use your 4283 Express Lane address. For UK online stores, use your Unit 9 Skyport Drive address.

Where is my package?

We'll email you when a package has been logged into your suite, and again once a shipment has been prepared with tracking information. You can check status anytime in Order History.

Why do I need to enter my merchandise values?

Customs and US Census Bureau regulations require accurate prices for export. We use the merchant invoice in your package; if there isn't one, we'll ask you to confirm values before shipping.

How can I estimate international shipping costs?

Use our ship rate calculator. Ask the merchant for the weight and dimensions of the package and plug them in.

How do I ship my packages?

Depending on your Shipping Preferences:

  • Automatic — Sent as soon as packages arrive.
  • Weekly / Bi-Weekly / Monthly — Sent on a schedule.
  • Hold — You submit a ship request to consolidate & send.
Can MyUS ship my products?

We can ship nearly every item — clothing, cosmetics, toys, cell phones, laptops, and much more. Items restricted by the US government or banned in your destination cannot be shipped.
